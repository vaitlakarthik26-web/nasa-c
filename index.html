PROJECT BRIEF / ABOUT

A museum beyond Earth.

Abandoned but not Forgotten is an educational space-heritage experience about the human-made objects that remain on the Moon and Mars. It combines interactive planetary globes, reconstructed field environments and historical context to help visitors understand how exploration works—and why equipment is sometimes left behind.

THE PROBLEM

Mission history is hard to visualize

Mission records, landing hardware and scientific experiments are spread across technical archives. A visitor may know the mission name without understanding what physically remains at the site.

THE SOLUTION

Explore artifacts in context

The project turns mission records into an interactive museum with planet selection, equipment dossiers, a chronological timeline and first-person 3D exploration.

TECHNOLOGY

Browser-based 3D

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js and WebGL power the experience. Procedural scene elements are clearly identified as reconstructions.

EDUCATIONAL VALUE

Fact, model and status

The archive separates confirmed historical facts from illustrative models and distinguishes inactive hardware from active missions.
